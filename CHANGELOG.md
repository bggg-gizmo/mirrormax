# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Restored the Desert Dusk sample and original mirror marks, with a brighter slime-green highlight over near-black and purple surfaces.
- Fixed a preview resize issue that could briefly show the wrong region of the frame when the canvas changed size.
- Fixed source audio dropping when the first AAC packet had a negative presentation timestamp.
- Verified PNG, GIF, and MP4 outputs and Android MediaStore placement across eight cases on an Android 11 emulator.
- Published the signed APK, SHA-256, complete source, and build and test documentation.

## 1.0.0

- Added two independent horizontal and vertical mirror passes with directional, Both sides, and Mirror all controls.
- Added portrait and landscape layouts, a looping preview, original comparison, and a built-in sample.
- Added still-image, GIF, MP4, and MOV imports with PNG, animated GIF, and MP4 export as appropriate.
- Added optional video sound, the Downloads/MirrorMax/Exports folder, and in-app opening and sharing of exports.
